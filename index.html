Ilyeo Lee is a product designer based in Seoul. She works at the point where AI meets everyday products, designing when and how a system should step in, and building the interfaces and design systems that make it usable.

She enjoys vibe coding, building what she designs to test it for real. She has developed research, design system and user test tools, product simulators, and this website herself with Claude Code.

She shipped three apps with the IT community DEPROMEET. At Samsung Design Membership, she worked on projects for Samsung Electronics CDO and the MX Business, and designed the UX for award-winning concept products including Hotcake and COMONG.

She studies Visual Communication Design at Hongik University and previously worked as a product designer at Bunjang.
